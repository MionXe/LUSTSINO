# LUSTSINO — Adult-compatible publisher networks (research, not approval)

The current site has clearly labeled advertising placeholders. No live ad tags have been installed. A network must approve the exact domain and intended content before you add its code.

## Suggested shortlist
1. **ExoClick** — https://www.exoclick.com/publishers/ — adult-compatible inventory including responsive banners, native and video placements. Official compliance info: https://www.exoclick.com/trust-and-safety/
2. **JuicyAds** — https://www.juicyads.com/Publishers/ — banners and native-style ad options; their published guidance warns against ad farms.
3. **TrafficStars** — https://trafficstars.com/publishers — adult industry ads; site/source approval required before activating ad spots.

## Suggested placement plan
- One responsive banner *after* the featured game selector.
- One clearly marked native/display ad after several vertical feed items, never camouflaged as a playable card.
- Optional desktop sidebar ad on non-game pages.
- Do not interrupt a timed game, auto-redirect, open popunders, or add forced notification subscriptions.
- Do not place ads beside unverified user uploads or age-restricted material until the relevant compliance controls are operating.

## Policy checklist
- Approval for adult interactive gaming content (this site is a game experience, not real-money gambling).
- Verify payment eligibility and country availability with each provider.
- Age verification/assurance appropriate for user locations, user-upload moderation, proof of performers' age and rights, complaint/takedown system, and required 2257 disclosures where applicable.
- Site host must allow the intended content. See Vercel's current AUP: https://vercel.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy .
- Banner creatives and user controls should not imply an ad is content or a game reward.
- Use consent/cookie controls required for each visitor's jurisdiction before adding tracking scripts.

**Do not claim approval or payment eligibility until the network confirms both.**
