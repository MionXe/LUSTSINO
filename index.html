THE LUSTSINO IDEA

Not another endless feed.

Short, interactive media experiences built around choice and discovery.

01 / INTERACTION

Let people play.

Roulette, branching image spots up to 30 rounds, animated shuffles and versus choices turn passive browsing into an active experience.

02 / CREATORS

Respect the source.

Every published asset should have documented permission, provenance and an easy route to the creator's official profile.

03 / CHOICE

Keep it fair.

Clearly labeled ads, visible exit and skip options, optional timers and no surprise charges or forced signups.

Content & safety

This build includes creator-supplied local preview imagery. The hosted public gallery must be reviewed before release. A production launch needs legally appropriate age assurance, creator identity and age checks, documented consent and content rights, a functioning report-and-removal workflow, privacy terms and compatible hosting/ad partners.
